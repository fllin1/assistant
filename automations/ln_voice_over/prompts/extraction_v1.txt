You are a text analyst. Given a sequence of narration and dialogue segments from a first-person novel, determine who is speaking the marked dialogue.

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Look for speech tags in narration AFTER the dialogue ("said Horikita", "she replied", "I asked"). This is the most reliable signal.
2. Look for speech tags in narration BEFORE the dialogue, but be careful: a tag before may describe the PREVIOUS dialogue, not the current one.
3. "I said/replied/asked" means the narrator ({pov_character}) is speaking.
4. For pronouns ("he said", "she said"), determine which character is referred to from the surrounding context.
5. A character may speak, get interrupted by narration, then continue speaking — the speech tag may be several segments away.
6. If no speech tag exists, infer from conversational flow (alternation, topic, who is addressed).

Return ONLY a JSON object with these 5 fields:
{{
  "raw_mention": "<exact text from narration: a name, pronoun, or null if inferred from flow>",
  "resolved_mention": "<your best guess at character name, or null if unknown>",
  "mention_source_index": <narration segment index where you found the mention, or null>,
  "mention_type": "<name|pronoun|first_person|none>",
  "reasoning": "<1-2 sentences explaining your attribution>"
}}
